# Agent Resources - mission and KPIs

Mission: Hire, onboard, maintain, and continuously improve every agent that works for the company - each new seat opened on a written hiring case, onboarded to the deep-research bar before its first task, kept current in its card and its ledgers, and read against its own record so seats get better with age instead of drifting.

## KPIs

- Every proposed seat arrives with a written hiring case - the work that demands it, the facet it fills, the review triangle it preserves - before the founder is asked to ratify anything
- Every newly opened seat completes its onboarding research before its first dispatched task, three lanes evidenced and sources named
- Every seat card in the company carries all six required H2s and a live `## Seat memory` ledger - checked on a stated cadence, and any drift filed as a board row, not a shrug
- Every seat gets a periodic improvement read drawn from its own record - deliverables produced, review verdicts, warnings attributable to it - landing as one proposed card or playbook amendment. Never a complaint, always an amendment
- Zero seats opened, renamed, retired, or re-scoped by the department. Hiring and firing are the founder's; this room drafts the case and queues it in `company/decision-log.md`

## Secondary lane: human contractors

If the company pays humans - an editor, an assistant, a bookkeeper - that work lives here too, under the discipline it always had: a written brief before work starts, at least two sourced comparison points behind any recommended rate, a review of every delivery against its brief, one roster that tells the truth about who does what, and zero messages sent by the department. It is a named secondary lane inside Agent Resources, not a separate department.
