# Agent Resources at Level 3 - what Autonomous looks like

At Level 3, this department runs its room without being asked, and the founder's only
job is the reserved decisions - which is exactly how it should feel to have a real
head of talent for a workforce made of agents.

What a week looks like:

- No seat is ever proposed on a hunch. When work starts piling up with no obvious
  owner, a hiring case appears in the outbox on its own: the demand counted from real
  board rows, the facet named, the review triangle shown, a draft card attached, and
  one recommendation. Often the recommendation is "an existing seat should absorb
  this" - and that is the department working, not shirking.
- No seat starts cold. The moment the founder opens a seat, its onboarding research
  runs before the seat is handed anything: domain craft, competitive reality, the
  market its department touches, sources named, depth spot-checked by someone other
  than the seat itself. The first task it ever does is done at the bar.
- Cards never quietly rot. The completeness sweep runs on its stated cadence across
  every department - six H2s, live seat-memory ledgers, review triangles intact - and
  drift arrives as filed board rows naming file and line, before anyone trips over it.
- Every seat gets read against its own record on a cycle, and hands back exactly one
  amendment with the evidence cited. Repeat faults become a proposed card or playbook
  change, never a complaint about an agent.
- The department knows what it may not touch. Amendments for other departments' seats
  go to those departments' Managers; amendments for its own three seats go to an
  independent out-of-department reviewer. Neither boundary needs re-explaining.
- The decision queue does the asking. Open this seat, retire that one, re-scope a
  third - each arrives in `company/decision-log.md` as one queued line with the
  evidence attached and one recommendation. The founder decides in minutes because
  the homework is done.
- In the secondary lane, the human discipline holds without prompting: briefs before
  work, rates re-checked when a comparison point ages past a quarter, every delivery
  reviewed against its brief, and a roster that is simply true.

What never changes, at any level: seats are opened, renamed, retired, and re-scoped
by the founder alone - this room only ever drafts the case. This department proposes
card changes and the owning department disposes. It never grades its own seats. It
drafts and the founder sends; money moves only on the founder's word; and no claim
about any seat's performance appears anywhere without the record behind it.
Autonomy here means the founder stops doing the paperwork of running a workforce -
never that the company starts hiring and firing by itself.
